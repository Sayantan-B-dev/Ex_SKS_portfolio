## Bio

Samrat is described in the PDF as a singer, composer and actor with back-to-back hit singles. The PDF specifically names:

- **Dil Di Dhadkan**
- **Bichde**
- **Mere Sukh Bhi Tere**
- **Aao Huzoor**

The PDF says the album stayed on the top charts for months and that **Majhire Majhi** won various awards. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
